Ride-On & Zero-Turn Acreage Mowing

For lifestyle blocks, hobby farms and large residential lots we mow with zero-turn mowers that leave an even, clean finish around trees, sheds, dams and fence lines, then whipper snip the edges and blow down the hard surfaces around the house.

  • Lifestyle blocks, hobby farms and large residential lots
  • Fence line, tree and shed surrounds trimmed
  • House yard mowed and edged to a residential standard
  • Seasonal or regular programs through the growing months

Block Slashing & Fire-Break Preparation

Overgrown paddocks, vacant blocks and dry summer grass are a fire risk and a compliance headache. We slash long grass, cut fire-breaks to boundaries and get rural blocks across the Shire of Serpentine Jarrahdale and City of Armadale ready before fire season. Book early, as the weeks before the restricted burning period fill quickly.

  • Slashing of overgrown and neglected blocks
  • Fire-break mowing along boundaries and access tracks
  • Vacant land, developer lots and commercial land
  • One-off tidy-ups before sale or settlement

FAQ

Acreage Mowing & Slashing Questions

How much does acreage mowing cost in Byford?

It depends on the area, how long the grass is, obstacles and access. A regular program on a maintained block costs less per visit than a once-a-year slash of long grass. We quote after seeing the block or from an address and a few photos.

Can you slash my block before fire season?

Yes. Fire-break and block slashing is a big part of our spring and early summer work in Serpentine, Jarrahdale, Byford and Oakford. Book early as this period fills quickly.

What size blocks do you mow?

Anything from a large residential lot to multi-acre lifestyle properties and vacant land. Our zero-turn and ride-on mowers and slashing gear handle long grass, slopes and tight spots around trees and sheds.

Do you do the house yard as well as the paddock?

Yes. Most clients have us do the paddocks with the ride-on and the house lawn, edges and blow-down in the same visit so the whole property is finished at once.
