Hedge Trimming & Pruning

Lilly pillies, viburnum, murraya, box and conifer hedges all respond to regular shaping. We trim to a clean line, prune shrubs and small trees for shape and health, and tidy palms so fronds and seed pods are not dropping on the lawn.

  • Privacy and boundary hedge trimming

  • Palm trimming and frond removal
  • All trimmings removed from site

Weeding, Mulching & Garden Beds

A thick layer of mulch keeps the soil cool, cuts watering and stops most weeds before they start. We weed beds properly, apply mulch, supply and plant new stock to suit WA conditions and build or rework garden beds and edging.

  • Weed removal and selective weed control
  • Mulch supply and spreading
  • Plant supply and planting
  • New garden beds and edging

FAQ

Garden Maintenance & Hedging Questions

What does garden maintenance in Perth cost?

It depends on the size of the garden, how overgrown it is and how often you want us back. A regular monthly or six-weekly program is cheaper per visit than a one-off catch-up. We quote after seeing the garden or from photos.

Do you take the green waste away?

Yes. All hedge trimmings, prunings and weeds are removed from site unless you would rather keep them for your own composting.

How often should hedges be trimmed in Perth?

Most fast-growing hedges such as lilly pilly and viburnum need trimming three to four times a year, with the main cuts in spring and late summer. Slower hedges like box can be done twice a year.

Can you combine garden maintenance with lawn mowing?

Yes, and most clients do. Mowing, edging, hedging and bed work are done in the same visit so the whole property is finished together.

Do you do end-of-lease garden clean-ups?

Yes. We bring overgrown rental gardens back to a handover standard, including mowing, edging, weeding, hedging and rubbish removal, across the South-East corridor.
