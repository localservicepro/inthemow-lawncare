What We Do in Each Area

South-East Perth

The established suburbs along Albany Highway and Tonkin Highway: family homes on older, larger blocks, rental properties and strata complexes. Regular lawn mowing, hedging, garden maintenance and reticulation repairs are the bread and butter here, with commercial and strata programs in Canning Vale, Thornlie and Armadale.

Suburbs: Canning Vale, Thornlie, Southern River, Huntingdale, Gosnells, Maddington, Kelmscott, Seville Grove, Armadale, Mount Richon

Byford growth corridor

Home turf. New estates in Byford, Hilbert, Haynes, Wandi and Treeby mean a lot of new turf installs, retic set-ups and first-year lawn establishment, while Oakford, Cardup and Mundijong bring larger blocks that need ride-on mowing and seasonal slashing.

Suburbs: Byford, Hilbert, Haynes, Cardup, Mundijong, Whitby, Oakford, Wandi, Anketell, Casuarina

Serpentine / Hills

Lifestyle blocks, hobby farms and bush properties on the scarp. Acreage mowing, paddock slashing and fire-break preparation ahead of the restricted burning period are the big jobs, along with keeping house lawns and gardens tidy around the homestead.

Suburbs: Serpentine, Jarrahdale, Bedfordale, Roleystone, Karragullen

Suburbs: Baldivis, Waikiki, Warnbro, Port Kennedy, Secret Harbour, Golden Bay

FAQ

Service Area Questions

My suburb is not on the list. Can you still help?

Probably. The list covers the suburbs we visit most, but if you are close to one of them, call 0424 058 261 and we will tell you straight away whether we can fit you in.

Is there a travel charge for Mandurah or the hills?

Regular customers in every region are quoted as a flat per-visit price with no separate travel charge. One-off jobs a long way from Byford may be grouped with other work in the area so the price stays fair, and we will tell you if timing is affected.

Do you service commercial and strata sites across the whole area?

Yes. Scheduled commercial and strata lawn maintenance is available in every region we cover, including Canning Vale, Armadale, Cockburn Central, Rockingham and Mandurah.
